Oceanic Feeling

"a sense of eternity; a feeling of being one with the external world as a whole." The Oceanic Feeling series contains 10 animations and is part of the poetics surrounding the search for a sense of wonder in the face of the rupture of the ego. Through the submersion of the sense of sight, the series evokes a sense of immensity, even at sea level.
